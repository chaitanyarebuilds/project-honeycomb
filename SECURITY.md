# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security problems.

- Use GitHub's private reporting: the **Report a vulnerability** button on this repository's Security tab, or
- Email chaitanyapandita@wolfrontechnologies.com with the subject line "SECURITY: Project Honeycomb".

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and its likely impact. We aim to acknowledge reports within 5 working days and will keep you updated until the issue is resolved. With your permission, we will credit you in the fix.

## Scope

In scope: code, configurations and designs in this repository, including design flaws that would cause harm if built as described.

Out of scope: vulnerabilities in third-party products. Report those to their vendors, or to CERT-In for issues affecting Indian systems.

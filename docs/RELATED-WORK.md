## What we think is still missing

- One system covering the whole loop, from buying time to shared memory, with brakes built in.
- An immune system for an organisation's own AI agents.
- Affordable managed protection for government offices and small firms, with Indian reporting rules built in.

Prove us wrong: that is the point of this page.
